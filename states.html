Passbook Design system

State components

Three lightweight, reusable cards for waiting and error states. Amber for "waiting on you or someone else," plain hairline for neutral information, green for something actively being recorded. Red is reserved for real failures — it never appears for ordinary waiting.

Wallet not connected

Used on the dashboard if a session drops or a wallet disconnects mid-visit.

⚠ Your wallet's disconnected — reconnect to approve expenses.

Amber, not red: this isn't a failure, it's a normal thing that happens between sessions. Pair with a one-tap reconnect action where it appears inline.

Co-signer hasn't joined yet

Used when a treasurer tries to submit an expense before all three signers have accepted their invite.

ⓘ Waiting for Arjun to connect their wallet before you can submit expenses.

Neutral hairline card, not amber — nothing is wrong, the club just isn't fully set up yet. Names the specific person, not "a co-signer," so the treasurer knows exactly who to nudge.

Transaction pending

Used right after a request, approval, or account action is submitted, before it's recorded.

Your request is being recorded — this usually takes a few seconds.

Green, not amber — this is progress, not a wait on a person. The spinner respects reduced-motion (swaps to a static dot); never block the rest of the screen while this shows.

True failure (for reference)

Reserved for real errors — a rejected transaction, an invalid amount, a wallet error. Not used for any of the three states above.

✕ That request couldn't be recorded — try again, or check your wallet connection.

Says what happened and what to try next. Never vague, never apologetic.
